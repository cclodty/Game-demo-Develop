# 每晚都見到的過場：交 ChatGPT 的版本

`docs/storyboard/02-every-night.md` 的分鏡係俾人睇的；呢份係由分鏡的提示詞整理出嚟、可以逐段直接貼的版本（點解唔交成份分鏡，見 `01-prologue-chatgpt.md`）。分鏡一改，呢份要跟住改（由 Claude 一齊改）。

## 用法

1. **最好用返畫序章嗰個 ChatGPT 對話**，人物同場景先會一致；嗰個對話冇咗，就開一個新的，附上序章的圖。
2. 貼「第 0 段：設定」，附上 **9527 人物設定圖**同**序章第 2、4、5、7、8 頁**。ChatGPT 應該只答「ready」，唔畫圖。
3. 之後每段貼一頁，附上嗰段標題寫明的圖。一段一張圖。
4. 邊頁唔啱，就喺同一個對話講：「重畫第 N 頁：……（邊度唔啱）」。三張層卡要同一個版面：第 2、3 頁出嚟同第 1 頁唔似，就叫佢「跟返第 1 頁的版面重畫」。
5. 存檔：`night-1.png` 至 `night-4.png`，交返俾 Claude。

## 第 0 段：設定（附：9527 設定圖、序章第 2、4、5、7、8 頁）

```text
I am making more comic-panel cutscenes for the same video game as the prologue you drew. This set is 4 pages: three short "act" cards that open each act of a night, and one panel shown every time the hero dies. I will send one page per message; you draw exactly one image for each page. Do not draw anything for this message: just reply "ready" and wait for page 1.

The rules for every image (the same as the prologue):
A 16:9 widescreen landscape image (wider than tall) showing one page of a game cutscene made of comic panels: the panels laid out across a dark textured background, with slanted, angled borders and thick black gutters. Modern Japanese anime style: bold flat colours, clean two-tone cel shading, crisp black ink outlines, halftone screentone in the shadows, dynamic camera angles, speed lines on action. A fictional night city with a Hong Kong / Macau flavour; no real-world landmarks or recognisable buildings. Black-humour action-comedy mood. Original characters that do not resemble any real person or actor. No speech bubbles, no captions, no letters, words, numbers or logos anywhere: the game adds all the words.

The characters and places (the attached pages are from the prologue; keep everyone and everything looking the same):
- 9527, a skinny, harmless-looking film extra in his late twenties: soft round face, sleepy droopy eyes, a blank, dazed, slightly lost expression with his mouth a little open, messy black hair with a cowlick sticking up, slightly hunched, a cheap white suit one size too big with sleeves too long. He looks innocent and bewildered, like someone things just happen to; never fierce, never cool, never frowning. The blank, dazed look is only his resting face: when a page names another expression (terror, a smile), draw that expression clearly instead. The attached character sheet is him: keep his face, hair and suit exactly the same.
- The crew woman: in her forties, a cap, a grey work shirt, tired, never looking up. She snaps the clapperboard at the start of every act.
- The show's director and host: a scruffy, heavy-set man in his fifties in a loud floral shirt, a cap, headphones around his neck, a megaphone in his hand.
- The gamblers: wealthy people in suits and evening dresses, wearing masquerade masks. They never appear in person: only inside video-call windows on screens, from their own luxury rooms, yachts and private clubs.
- The gang boss: never shown. Only his hand with chunky gold rings, the flame of his lighter or cigar, and a grinning row of gold teeth in the shadows.
- The King of the Underworld and the kind old woman who serves soup at the reincarnation counter: as in the prologue's underworld pages.
- The bamboo theatre: the huge traditional Cantonese opera theatre of bamboo scaffolding from the prologue, with giant video screens lashed to its frame and rows of empty seats.
```
