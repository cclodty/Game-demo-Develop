# 頭目入場：交 ChatGPT 的版本

`docs/storyboard/03-bosses.md` 的分鏡係俾人睇的；呢份係由分鏡的提示詞整理出嚟、可以逐段直接貼的版本（點解唔交成份分鏡，見 `01-prologue-chatgpt.md`）。分鏡一改，呢份要跟住改（由 Claude 一齊改）。

## 用法

1. **最好用返畫序章同層卡嗰個 ChatGPT 對話**；嗰個對話冇咗，就開一個新的，附上序章的圖。
2. 貼「第 0 段：設定」，附上 **9527 人物設定圖**。ChatGPT 應該只答「ready」，唔畫圖。
3. 貼「第 1 段」出**六個頭目的設定圖**；之後每頁都附上呢張。
4. 之後每段貼一頁，附上嗰段標題寫明的圖。一段一張圖。五頁要同一個版面：出嚟唔似第 1 頁，就叫佢「跟返第 1 頁的版面重畫」。
5. 邊頁唔啱，就喺同一個對話講：「重畫第 N 頁：……（邊度唔啱）」。
6. 存檔：設定圖 `bosses-sheet.png`，入場頁 `boss-1.png` 至 `boss-5.png`，交返俾 Claude。

## 已經畫咗？重畫第 2–5 頁（10-30 第二稿）

設定圖同第 1 頁（刀姐）照用。第一稿第 2–5 頁的背景係原型茶樓的四個房，好違和；第二稿每個頭目一個自己的片場。

**最易：上載一個檔。** 喺同一個對話上載 [`03-bosses-redraw.txt`](03-bosses-redraw.txt)（純文字，入面已經有更正同第 2–5 頁），ChatGPT 會先畫第一張，之後每張打 `next`；邊張唔啱就打 `redo` 加一句點改。唔好上載呢份 `.md`，亦唔好貼 GitHub 連結：開發者 10-30 試過，ChatGPT 讀唔到。

想逐段貼，就照下面：喺**同一個對話**先貼下面這段，再貼第 3、4、5、6 段（即第 2–5 頁），每段附返標題寫明的圖：

```text
Change of plan for the boss pages. Keep the lineup sheet and page 1 (the action director in the restaurant hall) exactly as they are: they are right. Redraw pages 2, 3, 4 and 5: their backgrounds were wrong, because every boss now fights in their own location, which matches their film department: the pyrotechnics chief at a container port, the set security chief at the toll plaza in front of an undersea road tunnel (the only road out of the city), the cinematographer and the casting director on a film sound stage, and the producer in the bamboo theatre (with only ordinary henchmen behind him, never the other bosses). All at night. Do not draw anything for this message: just reply "ok", then I will send pages 2, 3, 4 and 5 again.
```

## 第 0 段：設定（附：9527 設定圖）

```text
I am making more comic-panel cutscenes for the same video game as the prologue and the act cards you drew. This set is the bosses' entrances: first one character lineup sheet of the six bosses, then 5 pages, one per boss fight. Each page has the same layout: a big panel of the boss on the left, the boss's line top right, the hero's reaction bottom right. I will send one image request per message; you draw exactly one image for each. Do not draw anything for this message: just reply "ready" and wait.

The rules for every page (the same as before):
A 16:9 widescreen landscape image (wider than tall) showing one page of a game cutscene made of comic panels: the panels laid out across a dark textured background, with slanted, angled borders and thick black gutters. Modern Japanese anime style: bold flat colours, clean two-tone cel shading, crisp black ink outlines, halftone screentone in the shadows, dynamic camera angles, speed lines on action. A fictional night city with a Hong Kong / Macau flavour; no real-world landmarks or recognisable buildings. Black-humour action-comedy mood. Original characters that do not resemble any real person or actor. No speech bubbles, no captions, no letters, words, numbers or logos anywhere: the game adds all the words.

Every boss's first panel is also shown alone later, with a name plate the game adds in its lower left corner: keep the lower left of that panel darker and free of important detail.

The characters (keep everyone looking the same on every page):
- 9527, a skinny, harmless-looking film extra in his late twenties: soft round face, sleepy droopy eyes, a blank, dazed, slightly lost expression with his mouth a little open, messy black hair with a cowlick sticking up, slightly hunched, a cheap white suit one size too big with sleeves too long. He looks innocent and bewildered, like someone things just happen to; never fierce, never cool, never frowning. The blank, dazed look is only his resting face: when a page names another expression (terror, a smile), draw that expression clearly instead. The attached character sheet is him: keep his face, hair and suit exactly the same.
- The six bosses are the crew of a gang's deadly reality show, each one a film department: the action director (blades and storyboard sheets), the pyrotechnics chief (a machine gun and explosive crates), the set security chief (a tower shield and a walkie-talkie), the cinematographer (a sniper rifle whose scope looks like a camera lens), the casting director (a shotgun and headshot photos, from the prologue) and the producer, the gang boss (gold teeth, gold rings, a cigar). Their looks are in the lineup sheet I will ask for first; after that, keep them exactly as in that sheet.
- Each boss fights in their own location, which matches their film department: the action director in a grand old Chinese restaurant hall, the pyrotechnics chief at a container port, the set security chief at the toll plaza in front of an undersea road tunnel (the only road out of the city), the cinematographer and the casting director on a film sound stage, and the producer in the bamboo theatre from the prologue. All the entrance pages are at night.
- The gamblers never appear in person: only inside video-call windows on screens.
- The bamboo theatre: the huge traditional Cantonese opera theatre of bamboo scaffolding from the prologue, with giant video screens lashed to its frame and rows of empty seats.
```
