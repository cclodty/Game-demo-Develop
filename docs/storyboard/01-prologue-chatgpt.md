# 序章：交 ChatGPT 的版本

`docs/storyboard/01-prologue.md` 的分鏡係俾人睇的（有中文表格、對白、說明），**唔好成份交俾 ChatGPT**：佢會嘗試將表格入面的中文對白畫入圖（中文字好易畫錯），亦可能一次過畫幾頁、或者撈亂頁數。呢份係由分鏡的提示詞整理出嚟、可以逐段直接貼的版本；分鏡一改，呢份要跟住改（由 Claude 一齊改）。

## 用法

1. 開一個**新的** ChatGPT 對話，全部八頁都喺同一個對話入面畫，人物先會一致。
2. 貼「第 0 段：設定」，附上 **9527 人物設定圖**同**之前試畫的地府圖**。ChatGPT 應該只答「ready」，唔畫圖。
3. 之後每段貼一頁，每段都附上 9527 人物設定圖（第 7、8 頁再附埋地府圖）。一段一張圖。
4. 邊頁唔啱，就喺同一個對話講：「重畫第 N 頁：……（邊度唔啱）」。
5. 存檔：`prologue-1.png` 至 `prologue-8.png`，交返俾 Claude。如果出嚟唔係 16:9（例如 3:2），照交就得，Claude 會處理。

## 第 0 段：設定（附：9527 設定圖、地府試畫圖）

```text
I am making the comic-panel cutscenes for a video game. I will send you the 8 pages of the prologue, one page per message, and you draw exactly one image for each page. Do not draw anything for this message: just reply "ready" and wait for page 1.

The rules for every image:
A 16:9 widescreen landscape image (wider than tall) showing one page of a game cutscene made of comic panels: the panels laid out across a dark textured background, with slanted, angled borders and thick black gutters. Modern Japanese anime style: bold flat colours, clean two-tone cel shading, crisp black ink outlines, halftone screentone in the shadows, dynamic camera angles, speed lines on action. A fictional night city with a Hong Kong / Macau flavour; no real-world landmarks or recognisable buildings. Black-humour action-comedy mood. Original characters that do not resemble any real person or actor. No speech bubbles, no captions, no letters, words, numbers or logos anywhere: the game adds all the words.

The characters (keep each one looking the same on every page):
- 9527, a skinny, harmless-looking film extra in his late twenties: soft round face, sleepy droopy eyes, a blank, dazed, slightly lost expression with his mouth a little open, messy black hair with a cowlick sticking up, slightly hunched, a cheap white suit one size too big with sleeves too long. He looks innocent and bewildered, like someone things just happen to; never fierce, never cool, never frowning. The attached character sheet is him: keep his face, hair and suit exactly the same.
- Chan, the casting director: a thin man in his forties, slicked-back hair, tinted glasses, a brown leather jacket and gloves, always holding a stack of headshot photos.
- The show's director and host: a scruffy, heavy-set man in his fifties in a loud floral shirt, a cap, headphones around his neck, a megaphone in his hand.
- A crew woman: in her forties, a cap, a clipboard, tired, never looking up.
- The gamblers: wealthy people in suits and evening dresses, wearing masquerade masks, holding betting slips and phones.
- The gang: men in dark suits with pistols. Their boss appears only as a shadowy figure whose gold teeth glint.
- The King of the Underworld: as in the attached underworld image (young-looking, long black hair, black official robes with gold embroidery, a tall black official hat).
- Meng Po: a kind old woman with grey hair and an apron.
- The heavenly emperor: an old man with a long white beard, golden robes and a golden crown, seen on a TV screen.
- The bamboo theatre: a huge traditional Cantonese opera theatre built of bamboo scaffolding with a corrugated iron roof, red and gold decorations and paper lanterns, on an empty lot between old tenement blocks, with giant video screens lashed to its bamboo frame.
```
