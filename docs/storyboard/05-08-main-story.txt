HOW TO USE THIS FILE

This file holds 20 image requests for the comic-panel cutscenes we have been making in this conversation: the rest of the main story. All the earlier reference images are already in this conversation above.

The story has changed. Jing Jing, the young film star, is now the true villain. Two months ago, in her first leading role, the hero played an extra who is shot behind her, and he played the helplessness of a man who has to die but cannot let go so sincerely that the director kept the take, and the focus of the shot fell on him instead of her. The city laughed at her. Out of jealousy and anger she flattered the gang boss into making the hero the prey of his deadly reality show, and she makes no secret of it. She is beautiful but unforgivable, and she is no fighter: she shoots from behind and lets others fight for her. In the true ending nobody exposes anyone: the hero bet his whole night's pay on himself surviving until dawn at 500 to 1, he is the only winner, and when he politely thanks Jing Jing on camera for choosing him, the gang boss decides she betrayed him. The hero survives the night: on the last page he appears only on the underworld's television. If you drew any of these pages before, the requests marked CHANGED or NEW replace them; the ones marked unchanged are the same as before.

1. Draw exactly ONE image per reply, in the order below. Start with REQUEST 1 now.
2. After each image, stop and wait. When I write "next", draw the next request. When I write "skip", do not draw the next request: pass over it and wait again (the request after it becomes the next one). When I write "redo" and a note, redraw the last image with that change.
3. Never put two requests into one image, and never draw any words from this file into an image.
